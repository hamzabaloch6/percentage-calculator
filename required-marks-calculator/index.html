How to Use the Required Marks Calculator

  1. Enter the total marks of your exam.
  2. Enter the percentage you want to achieve.
  3. Click the Calculate button.
  4. The calculator will show the minimum marks required.

Required Marks Formula

The formula used to calculate required marks is:

Required Marks = (Total Marks × Desired Percentage) ÷ 100

For example, if your exam has 500 total marks and you want 80%:

(500 × 80) ÷ 100 = 400 marks

Example

Suppose your exam is worth 600 marks and your target is 75%. You need:

(600 × 75) ÷ 100 = 450 marks

Therefore, you need at least 450 marks to achieve 75%.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a Required Marks Calculator?

It calculates the minimum marks you need to achieve a specific percentage in an exam.

Can I use this for any total marks?

Yes. You can enter any total marks and your desired percentage.

Does the calculator round the result?

Yes. The result is rounded up to the next whole mark when necessary.
