How to use the GPA Calculator

  1. Enter the credit hours for each course.
  2. Select the letter grade you received.
  3. Enter the courses you want to include.
  4. Tap Calculate GPA.
  5. Your weighted GPA will appear instantly.

GPA calculation formula

GPA = Σ (Grade Points × Credit Hours) ÷ Σ Credit Hours

The calculator uses a standard 4.0 grade-point scale. Your GPA is weighted according to the credit hours of each course.

A+ / A: 4.0
A-: 3.7
B+: 3.3
B: 3.0
B-: 2.7
C+: 2.3
C: 2.0
C-: 1.7
D+: 1.3
D: 1.0
F: 0.0

Frequently asked questions

What is GPA?
GPA stands for Grade Point Average. It represents your average academic performance using grade points.

How is GPA calculated?
Each course's grade points are multiplied by its credit hours. The total is then divided by the total credit hours.

Is this GPA calculator based on a 4.0 scale?
Yes. This calculator uses a standard 4.0 grade-point scale. Universities may use different grading policies.

Can I calculate GPA for multiple subjects?
Yes. You can enter up to five courses and their credit hours.
