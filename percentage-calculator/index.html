Percentage Calculator

Calculate percentages quickly and accurately. Use this free calculator to find a percentage of a number, determine what percentage one value is of another, or calculate percentage increase or decrease.

How to use the percentage calculator

  1. Choose the type of percentage calculation you need.
  2. Enter the numbers in the fields provided.
  3. Tap Calculate to see the result instantly.

Percentage formulas

Percentage of a number: (Percentage ÷ 100) × Number
What percent is X of Y: (X ÷ Y) × 100
Percentage change: ((New value − Original value) ÷ |Original value|) × 100

Examples

20% of 500: (20 ÷ 100) × 500 = 100.

80 is what percent of 100: (80 ÷ 100) × 100 = 80%.

500 to 600: ((600 − 500) ÷ 500) × 100 = 20% increase.

Frequently asked questions

How do I calculate a percentage?
Divide the percentage by 100 and multiply it by the number. For example, 25% of 200 is 50.

How do I find what percentage one number is of another?
Divide the first number by the total and multiply by 100.

How do I calculate percentage increase or decrease?
Compare the difference between the new and original values with the original value, then multiply by 100.
