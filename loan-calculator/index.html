How to Use the Loan Calculator

  1. Enter the amount you want to borrow.

  3. Enter the loan term in years.
  4. Click the Calculate Loan button.
  5. The calculator will show your estimated monthly payment, total payment and total interest.

Loan Payment Formula

For a standard fixed-rate loan, the monthly payment is calculated using:

M = P × r × (1 + r)n ÷ ((1 + r)n − 1)

Where M is the monthly payment, P is the loan amount, r is the monthly interest rate, and n is the total number of monthly payments.

Example

Suppose you borrow 100,000 at an annual interest rate of 10% for 5 years.

The calculator estimates the monthly payment and shows the total amount you will repay over the loan term.

Note: Actual loan payments may differ because of taxes, fees, insurance, compounding methods, or lender-specific terms.

Frequently Asked Questions

What does a Loan Calculator calculate?

It estimates your monthly loan payment, total repayment amount and total interest.

Can I use this calculator for different loan amounts?

Yes. Enter the loan amount, interest rate and repayment term for your loan.

Is the result an exact loan quote?

No. It is an estimate based on the information entered and a standard fixed-rate loan formula.
