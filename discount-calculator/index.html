How to Use the Discount Calculator

  1. Enter the original price of the product.
  2. Enter the discount percentage.
  3. Click the Calculate Discount button.
  4. The calculator will show your discount amount, savings and final price.

Discount Formula

The discount amount is calculated using:

Discount Amount = Original Price × Discount Percentage ÷ 100

The final sale price is:

Final Price = Original Price − Discount Amount

Example

Suppose an item costs 5,000 and has a 20% discount.

Discount = 5,000 × 20 ÷ 100 = 1,000

Final Price = 5,000 − 1,000 = 4,000

So, you save 1,000 and pay 4,000.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I calculate a discount?

Multiply the original price by the discount percentage and divide by 100.

How do I find the final sale price?

Subtract the discount amount from the original price.

Can I calculate a 50% or 100% discount?

Yes. The calculator supports discount percentages from 0% to 100%.
