Grade Calculator

Use this free Grade Calculator to calculate your percentage and letter grade from marks obtained and total marks. Enter your marks and get your result instantly.

How to use the Grade Calculator

  1. Enter the marks you obtained.
  2. Enter the total possible marks.
  3. Tap Calculate Grade.
  4. Your percentage and letter grade will appear instantly.

Grade calculation formula

Percentage = (Marks Obtained ÷ Total Marks) × 100

After calculating the percentage, the calculator assigns a grade according to the following scale.

A+: 90% and above
A: 80% – 89.99%
B: 70% – 79.99%
C: 60% – 69.99%
D: 50% – 59.99%
F: Below 50%

Example

Marks obtained: 425
Total marks: 500

Percentage = (425 ÷ 500) × 100 = 85%

Grade = A

Frequently asked questions

How is my percentage calculated?
Your marks obtained are divided by the total marks and multiplied by 100.

What grade is 90%?
According to the grading scale used on this page, 90% or higher is A+.

Can I use this calculator for school and exams?
Yes. You can use it for any result where you know the marks obtained and the total marks.
