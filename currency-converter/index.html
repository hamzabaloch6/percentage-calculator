How to Use the Currency Converter

  1. Enter the amount you want to convert.
  2. Select the currency you are converting from.
  3. Select the currency you want to convert to.
  4. Click Convert.
  5. The calculator fetches the latest available published exchange rate and shows the converted amount.

Currency Conversion Formula

If the exchange rate represents how many units of the target currency equal one unit of the source currency:

Converted Amount = Amount × Exchange Rate

For example, if the fetched rate is 1 USD = 280 PKR, then:

100 USD × 280 = 28,000 PKR

Example

Suppose you want to convert 100 USD to PKR. The calculator automatically fetches the latest available published USD to PKR rate.

If the fetched rate is 1 USD = 280 PKR:

100 × 280 = 28,000 PKR

The actual result will change when the latest published exchange rate changes.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does this calculator use live exchange rates?

It uses the latest available published reference exchange rate from the Frankfurter API. These are not real-time intraday trading rates, so bank, card, cash-exchange, and market rates may differ.

Which currencies are supported?

The calculator currently includes USD, PKR, EUR, GBP, AED, SAR, INR, CAD, AUD and JPY.

Do I need to enter the exchange rate manually?

No. The calculator automatically requests the latest available published rate when you click Convert.

Why can the result differ from my bank or exchange service?

Banks, exchange services and card providers may use different rates and may also add spreads or fees.
