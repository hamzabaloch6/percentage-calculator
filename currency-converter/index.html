Currency Conversion Formula

If the exchange rate represents how many units of the target currency equal one unit of the source currency:

Converted Amount = Amount × Exchange Rate

For example, if 1 USD = 280 PKR, then:

100 USD × 280 = 28,000 PKR

Example

Suppose you want to convert 100 USD to PKR and the exchange rate is 1 USD = 280 PKR.

100 × 280 = 28,000 PKR

So, 100 USD would equal 28,000 PKR at that exchange rate.
