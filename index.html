How to Calculate a Percentage?

Use this formula:

Percentage result = (Percentage ÷ 100) × Number

Example: 20% of 500 = 100.

How to Calculate a Discount?

Discount amount:

Discount = (Discount % ÷ 100) × Original Price

Final price:

Final Price = Original Price − Discount

Example: 20% off $1000 = $200 discount, final price = $800.
