Attendance Calculator

Calculate your attendance percentage instantly. Enter the classes attended and total classes to find your current attendance and check how many future classes you need to attend to reach your target.

How to use the Attendance Calculator

  1. Enter the number of classes you attended.
  2. Enter the total number of classes.
  3. Enter your desired target attendance percentage.
  4. Tap Calculate Attendance.
  5. Your current attendance and required classes will appear.

Attendance calculation formula

Attendance % = (Classes Attended ÷ Total Classes) × 100

To reach a target attendance, the calculator determines how many consecutive classes you need to attend.

Example

Classes attended: 42
Total classes: 50
Current attendance: 84%

If your target is 90%, you need to attend additional classes until your attendance reaches the target.

Frequently asked questions

How is attendance percentage calculated?
Divide classes attended by total classes and multiply by 100.

Can I find how many classes I need to attend?
Yes. Enter your target attendance and the calculator will calculate the additional classes required.

Can I use this for school, college or university?
Yes. The calculator can be used for any class attendance calculation.
