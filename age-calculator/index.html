How to Use the Age Calculator

  1. Enter your date of birth.
  2. Select the date on which you want to calculate your age.
  3. Click the Calculate Age button.
  4. Your exact age in years, months and days will be displayed.

How Age Is Calculated

The calculator compares your date of birth with the selected calculation date. It accounts for the different number of days in each month and leap years.

Your age is displayed as completed years, months and days.

Example

If your date of birth is January 15, 2000 and the calculation date is January 15, 2026, your age is:

26 years, 0 months and 0 days

Frequently Asked Questions

What does the Age Calculator calculate?

It calculates your exact age in completed years, months and days.

Can I calculate my age on a past or future date?

Yes. You can select any date on or after your date of birth.

Does it account for leap years?

Yes. The calculator uses calendar dates and correctly handles leap years.
