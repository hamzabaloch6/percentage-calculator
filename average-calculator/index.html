Average Formula

The average is calculated by adding all the numbers together and dividing the total by the number of values.

Average = Sum of all numbers ÷ Number of values

Example

Suppose the numbers are: 10, 20, 30, 40, 50

Sum = 10 + 20 + 30 + 40 + 50 = 150

Number of values = 5

Average = 150 ÷ 5 = 30

Frequently Asked Questions

What is an average?

An average is a single value that represents the typical value of a group of numbers.

How is average calculated?

Add all the numbers together and divide the sum by the number of values.

Can I enter decimal numbers?

Yes. The calculator supports decimal numbers such as 10.5, 20.75, and 30.25.
