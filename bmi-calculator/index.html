BMI Formula

BMI is calculated by dividing weight in kilograms by height in meters squared.

BMI = Weight (kg) ÷ Height² (m²)

For example, if your weight is 70 kg and your height is 1.75 m:

70 ÷ (1.75 × 1.75) = 22.86

BMI Categories for Adults

BMI Category
Below 18.5 Underweight
18.5 – 24.9 Healthy Weight
25.0 – 29.9 Overweight
30.0 or above Obesity

Frequently Asked Questions

What is BMI?

BMI, or Body Mass Index, is a number calculated from a person's weight and height and is commonly used as a general screening measure.

Is BMI accurate for everyone?

No. BMI does not directly measure body fat and may not accurately reflect body composition for every person.

Can children use adult BMI categories?

Adult BMI categories should not be applied to children in the same way. Children and teenagers require age- and sex-specific BMI interpretation.
